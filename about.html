About Phones For You

Founded to give everyday mobile users a fairer deal, Phones For You is an independent UK mobile retailer focused on simple, affordable smartphone plans.

Our Story

Too many people get trapped in expensive 24-month or 36-month phone contracts with hidden price rises. We started Phones For You to offer transparent 12-month plans, honest advice, and competitive prices on both flagship and budget phones.

Why Shop With Us?

1. Short, Flexible Contracts: No multi-year lock-ins or surprise mid-contract price hikes

2. Wide choice of brands: From the latest Apple iPhone and Samsung Galaxy models to affordable Xiaomi and oppo handsets.

3. Fast UK Delivery: Free next-day tracked delivery on all monthly phone plans.

4. Friendly Support: Real customer service ready to help you choose the right phone for your budget.
